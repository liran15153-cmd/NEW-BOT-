# Product Map

The product is currently a local deterministic financial wellness bot brain. It is not yet a production banking or WhatsApp product.

## Current Jobs

- [[Current Product Behavior]]: answer demo cash-flow, purchase, and installment questions.
- [[Supported Intents]]: classify supported and unsupported user intents.
- [[User Experience Principles]]: keep responses calm, practical, and non-judgmental.
- [[Privacy Model]]: avoid fake employer visibility and fake real-data claims.

## Current User

The current user is an internal tester or early employee-style end user testing conversational behavior before real data infrastructure exists.

## Product Mental Model

The product is a personal financial conversation layer, not a bank, broker, lender, or employer dashboard. The assistant should help a person understand whether a near-term expense creates pressure, what installment payments do to monthly cash flow, and what information is missing before giving a useful answer.

The current product promise is deliberately narrow. A narrow promise is good here because it lets the team test whether the interaction is useful before adding sensitive infrastructure. A broad promise would push the team into fake intelligence, fake data access, and privacy risk.

## What Good Looks Like

A good answer says what the system knows, what it does not know, and what practical implication follows. It should not be a lecture. It should not be a generic financial education article. It should be a short, grounded answer that respects the user's privacy and uncertainty.
